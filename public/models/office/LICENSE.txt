Sunny Life adaptations:
- Resize texture maps to 512px and encode as WebP.
- Remove one desk assembly for an interactive replacement.
- Flatten hierarchy and merge compatible static meshes.
- Fit the office to the resident scale.
- Add a functional desk, cubicle partitions, public cafe and restroom.
- Generate navigation obstacles from actual mesh triangles.
