WORK AND NEEDS

Playable flow
- Home computer -> Find work -> choose one of three office jobs.
- Commute is queued behind a current manual activity and walks through the
  home doorway before changing scene.
- The supplied Isometric office model is used, with one desk replaced by an
  interactive cubicle. A public cafe and restroom are added alongside it.
- Office paths use PathFinding.js and a grid rasterized from the real model.
- Work, break, paid refreshments, restroom, early departure and end-of-shift
  settlement are persistent states. No external clock or background replay
  advances the simulation while the page is closed.

Economy
- Salary is prorated by actual working minutes, rounded once at settlement.
- Commute, rest, coffee, lunch and restroom time are unpaid.
- One settlement per game day; switching jobs cannot collect twice.
- Coffee costs 18, lunch costs 28. Coffee is limited to two a day and a
  90-game-minute interval. Lunch is limited to one purchase a day.
- Benefits accrue during the break, not at purchase.
- Two hours of continuous work increases fun/energy drain and stress.
- A break resets the continuous-work streak. Severe hunger/exhaustion
  automatically ends the shift.
- Build undo/redo applies only the edit's budget delta, preserving wages
  and other non-build transactions.
- Existing apartment/coastal residence budget separation remains unchanged.

Needs
- Healthy: above 50, green #57966a.
- Low: above 20 through 50, orange #c78c35.
- Urgent: 0 through 20, red #cb5c52.
- The same colors drive all six bars, mood status and the overhead marker.
- At bladder zero outside an active toilet action: one accident, hygiene=0,
  bladder=65, fun=-18, embarrassed mood for 90 game minutes.
- Accident count, time and mood duration are saved. Reload does not repeat it.
- Normal social deprivation reduces fun progressively. The Social trait
  amplifies that effect and increases social decay.
- Very low hygiene/hunger lowers fun; severe hunger also drains energy.
- Existing base need-decay rates are unchanged.
- These are fictional game mechanics, not medical or psychological guidance.

Implementation
- career.js: persistent career transitions, settlement and validation.
- needs.js: coupled needs, accident handling and semantic severity.
- office-layout.js: grid routing and interaction anchors.
- office.js: real-time visual motion, keyboard IK and cafe props.
- office-assets.js: lazy model loading, retry after failure.
- CareerPanel.jsx: job listings and compact career controls.
