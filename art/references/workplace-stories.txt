Sunny Life: workplace cast, projects and consequences

Source
- Adapted from the user-provided "NPC人物设定.md".
- All four characters are fictional game characters, not claims about real people.
- Visual appearances use existing licensed, animated resident models with distinct
  bodies, hair, clothes, glasses and name labels. The input supplies personalities,
  not exact visual reference images.
- The private local resident policy remains unchanged.

Roster and hierarchy
- Rank 1 / small company: Danbao.
- Rank 2 / medium company: Danbao, Shuijie, Zhuoge.
- Rank 3 / large company: all four, including Dayanzei.
- Player -> Danbao OR Shuijie -> Zhuoge -> Dayanzei.
- Team leader can be switched between shifts at rank 2+.
- The other team leader is a cross-team partner, not another direct supervisor.
- Existing legacy friend relationships remain in saves but are not office managers.

Project clock
- Each new shift receives one project with an initial 360 game minutes of work.
- Paid attendance is distinct from productive project progress. Required meetings
  and waiting do not earn project progress, but do not remove already earned salary.
- Three interruptions arrive after 45, about 155, and about 280 paid work minutes.
- The exact second/third checkpoint varies with the saved project number.
- A pending message consumes working time; after 30 unattended work minutes its
  explicitly defined default decision is taken once and recorded.
- Opening workplace/project/event dialogs pauses simulation for reading. The chosen
  meeting/coordination cost is applied as real game minutes after closing.
- At high stress, focused production slows to 80%.
- Report action: 10 minutes, at least 60 paid work minutes between reports.
- Submission is possible after 60% completion, takes 10 minutes, and locks the
  submitted artifact. Submitting early protects the scope but risks a poor review.

NPC behavior and knowledge
- Each NPC saves trust, closeness, interest, energy, hunger, mood, stress,
  reported progress, and a pending handoff.
- Trust, closeness and project interest are separate values. Closeness never removes
  a contribution-sharing condition.
- NPCs learn project progress only from direct assignment, a progress report,
  explicit escalation, or submission.
- High-level interventions require shared evidence of at least 20% progress.
- Danbao: explicit small tasks have a short delay; open-ended complex tasks have
  substantially longer delay. Fatigue, hunger, stress, mood and interest can delay work.
- Shuijie: real help and quick organization, but weak contribution records can
  transfer credit away from the player.
- Zhuoge: coordinates resources after demonstrated value, often with expanded scope.
- Dayanzei: first grants useful direction and resources, then gradually reveals
  uneven allocation and accountability over separate projects.
- Handoffs progress from promised -> real artifact -> player acceptance.
- Promises do not advance the player's task. Artifacts can be credited only once.
- One handoff and one follow-up per NPC per project prevent repeated reward farming.

Narrative progression
- Danbao: ambiguous direction, written options and responsibility boundaries.
- Shuijie / Zhuoge: shared ownership, acceptance criteria and scaling demands.
- Dayanzei's five stages occur at most once per project:
  vision -> real support -> biased allocation -> unequal standards -> informed approval.
- Each stage is driven by an observable decision, not a reveal in a character bio.
- Scope negotiation, written evidence, contribution ledgers and reporting affect the
  actual minutes, help, ownership, performance and job retention.
- Full event and choice journal is saved, capped at 60 entries; 20 reviews are retained.

Performance and dismissal
- Review weighs completion, quality, communication and evidence, then accounts for
  unhandled requests, missing submission and reduced contribution ownership.
- Score below 60 adds one warning. Score >=75 removes one warning.
- Health-forced departure is recorded but adds no warning.
- Repeated poor review requires at least three warnings and rolling performance
  below 55 before dismissal is proposed.
- Dismissal is not caused by one disagreement, refusal or relationship score.
- The proposal becomes an appeal event after returning home; it is not an immediate
  automatic deletion of employment. Pending appeal blocks departure and job switching.
- Evidence appeal requires both evidence and communication >=40. It preserves
  employment with two warnings, not a permanent guarantee.
- A one-time improvement plan can defer termination; further poor work can reopen it.
- Accepting termination preserves wages, savings, qualifications, home and appearance.
- Job search remains available. A new employment starts a fresh performance record,
  while narrative, relationship, prior-review and dismissal history remain saved.
- Recognition, reprimand and dismissal have time-limited mood effects. Dismissal lasts
  three game days and immediately lowers fun and raises stress.

Persistence and delivery
- Added career.workplace with legacy defaults; no online or offline auto-progress.
- A legacy office save credits existing shift work to the new project and skips
  interruption checkpoints that have already elapsed.
- Project/event IDs prevent duplicate decisions after reload or double click.
- Validation rejects malformed actors, references, task bounds and impossible casts.
- Local implementation; no commit, push or deployment is implied by test results.
