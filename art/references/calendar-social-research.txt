Research question
How can daily obligations and residual stress change a social encounter without
reducing friendship, attraction, and current mood to one interchangeable bar?

Signals examined
- Which person the action targets, and where their relationship is shown.
- Which interaction depends on relationship, current mood or mutual willingness.
- Whether group presence alone grants relationship progress.
- How negative and positive experiences coexist and expire.
- Whether dates can be ended, rescheduled, or repaired.

Assumption tested
Being with a romantic partner does not necessarily make the current encounter happy.

Finding that changes the design
Relationship satisfaction and current emotions can disagree; maintaining independent
state allows work frustration or a blackout to overshadow, not delete, affection.

Implementation decisions
- 28 game days per billing cycle; day 1 is Monday; weekday work and weekend rest.
- Rent is four times the salary at first lease/employment, fixed until housing changes.
  Default unemployed reference rent: 1,800, based on a 450 daily salary.
- Power 120, water 80, phone 40 and internet 60 per cycle before housing-size modifiers.
- Grace periods precede service cutoff; payment restores the actual affected controls.
- No forced eviction or deletion of a player's house for overdue rent.
- Invitation/party, physical arrival, target-specific interaction and departure are
  separate states. Parties cost 60; going out together costs 80.
- Friendship, romance, trust, resentment and satisfaction are independent.
- All romance candidates are adults. Relationship commitment requires mutual readiness.
- Mood sources carry strength and duration. Work stress, unmet needs and utility
  disconnection contribute to irritability and can provoke an optional conflict.
- Calm explanations, space and apologies provide different repair paths.
- A manual real-life reflection can add a mood source once per game day.
  No device state, private messages, health data or real-life events are read automatically.
- This is fictional gameplay tuning, not a model of mental health or a prediction of
  real people's feelings.
